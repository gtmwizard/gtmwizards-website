---
term: "Deliverability"
description: "The share of your emails that reach the inbox instead of spam — determined by infrastructure, volume discipline, and content quality."
---

Deliverability is won before the first email is sent: dedicated sending
domains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication, gradual warm-up, and volumes that
respect mailbox providers' limits. It is maintained by watching bounce
rates and reply health continuously. gtmWizards manages all of this on
hubsell, keeping your primary corporate domain out of the risk entirely.
