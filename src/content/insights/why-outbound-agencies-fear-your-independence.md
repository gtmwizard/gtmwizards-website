Every outbound agency faces the same conflict of interest: the better they
teach you, the sooner you leave. So the incentives push toward opacity —
proprietary "black box" tech you can't inspect, data you can't export,
playbooks that live in someone else's head.

gtmWizards is structured to escape that conflict. Our founders own
[hubsell](https://www.hubsell.com), the platform every campaign runs on.
Your lists, sequences, and results accumulate in your workspace from day
one. When you're ready to in-source, the handover is an account transfer,
not a rebuild.

That changes what "working with an agency" means:

- **Done for you** while you need speed.
- **Done with you** while your team learns on live campaigns.
- **Done by you** when the numbers say you're ready.

We think this is what the second generation of outbound services looks
like: partners who prove their technology instead of claiming it, and who
measure success by how little you eventually need them.
