---
term: "ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)"
description: "The precise definition of the companies most likely to buy, retain, and expand — the foundation of every outbound campaign."
---

Your ICP describes the companies worth pursuing: industry, size, geography,
tech stack, and the situational triggers that make now the right time. In
traditional industries, a useful ICP goes beyond firmographics — it names
the production realities, regulations, and buying committees that generic
databases can't filter for. gtmWizards builds lists against your ICP from
our own data operation, Kadanco, plus manual research.
